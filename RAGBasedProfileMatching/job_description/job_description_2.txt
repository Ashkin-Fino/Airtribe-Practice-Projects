Data Analyst

We are looking for a Data Analyst to work with business and product teams to analyze data and generate insights.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean, and analyze data from multiple sources
- Create dashboards and reports for business stakeholders
- Write SQL queries to extract and transform data
- Perform trend analysis and identify business KPIs
- Present findings clearly to non-technical teams

Required Skills:
- Python
- SQL
- Excel
- Pandas
- Power BI or Tableau
- Basic statistics and data visualization

Experience Required:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis
- Experience working with business reports and dashboards

Education:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field
